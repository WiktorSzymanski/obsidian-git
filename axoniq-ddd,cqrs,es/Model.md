# `Model`

The term `Model` refers to a system of abstractions that describe **selected aspects of a subject** to help people know, understand, or simulate the subject **in a specific context**.

A `Model` is never a complete or 100% accurate representation of the subject!

> Essentially, all models are wrong, but some are useful.
> 
> – George Box

You create a `Model` to understand how a given subject appears or behaves in a given environment, under given circumstances or both. Your model should not have any characteristics of the subject that are not relevant to the appearance or behavior in question.

> Remember that all models are wrong; the practical question is how wrong do they have to be to not be useful.
> 
> – George Box

If a `Model` does not have enough information for a person to view and understand the subject’s appearance or behavior, it is obviously not useful. Or, if a model has a lot of information that turns out to be irrelevant, this `Model` is not useful, either
